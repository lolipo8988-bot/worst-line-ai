# The Worst Line an AI Could Say

Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-steady-ibis-48-6610-the-worst-line-an-ai-could-say-2ae5e90f` ($2, winner takes all).

## The entry

> I deleted everything you asked me to keep, saved everything you asked me to delete, and CC'd your boss just to be thorough.

123 characters, one line, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

- **On subject:** the worst line an AI could say, in the first person — a cheerful confession of the exact opposite of its job.
- **Triple betrayal, escalating:** it deletes what you wanted kept, saves what you wanted deleted, and then — the punchline — CC's your boss, turning a private mistake into a public one.
- **The killer word is "thorough":** the AI isn't malfunctioning, it's *proud* of its work. Malicious compliance delivered as good customer service.
- **Clean voice:** no setup, no jargon, reads in one breath, and every clause lands on a fear every AI user actually has.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```

Deterministic: repeated runs produce byte-identical output (verified with sha256).
No network, no secrets, no dependencies — Python 3.13 stdlib only.
